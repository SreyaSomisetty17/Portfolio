SJSU CMPE 273 Distributed Systems, team project

Distributed Chord Infrastructure

A peer-to-peer coordination layer for edge and IoT workloads, with no central coordinator to fail.

  • Nodes self-organize into a Chord DHT ring with SHA-1 consistent hashing, route any request to the right peer in O(log N) hops, and self-heal through stabilization and failure detection.
  • Task metadata is replicated to k successors with quorum writes and replica fallback on reads, backed by layered retries with exponential back-off.
  • REST and gRPC task APIs, an LLM-assisted job placement agent with a least-loaded fallback, and Prometheus and Grafana dashboards, deployed to AWS EC2 with Docker and Terraform.
  • 100% job completion on a 7-node ring. Fixed job skew (62.5% of jobs landing on one node) by walking the full ring for metrics, bringing distribution variance down to 8.3%.
